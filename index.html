What we do

We provide an agentic AI solution purpose-built for small to mid-sized MDM implementations — the kind of program that used to require a six-figure consulting engagement and a year on the calendar. Singitham compresses that into a coordinated team of AI agents that source, clean, match, and publish your master data continuously, not once a quarter.

Instead of one monolithic tool, Singitham is organized as four specialized agents that hand work off to each other the way a well-run data team would: a sourcing agent that connects to your CRMs, ERPs, spreadsheets, and third-party feeds; a data quality agent that standardizes formats, fills gaps, and flags anomalies before they spread; an MDM agent that performs match-and-merge across systems to resolve duplicate customers, products, and vendors into a single trusted record; and a provisioning agent that pushes the golden record back out to every downstream system that depends on it.

Because the agents reason over your data rather than follow rigid rule sheets, Singitham adapts as your source systems change — new fields, new formats, new acquisitions — without months of re-mapping. It's MDM sized and priced for teams that don't have a dedicated data governance department, but still need data they can trust.

Use cases

MDM where trusted records drive revenue and compliance.

Across regulated and operationally complex industries, the hidden cost is usually the same: duplicate records, inconsistent hierarchies, and slow downstream decisions.

Finance

Customer, vendor, and account master data

Finance teams need a single view of counterparties and accounts to reduce risk, accelerate onboarding, and keep reporting accurate.

  • Unify customer and vendor records across CRM, ERP, and banking systems
  • Resolve duplicate legal entities for KYC, AML, and regulatory reporting
  • Standardize account hierarchies for cash, risk, and treasury workflows
Automobile

Vehicle, dealer, and parts master data

Automotive businesses often have fragmented dealer, supplier, and product records that slow warranty, service, and channel operations.

  • Match vehicle and VIN data across OEM, dealer, and service platforms
  • Clean supplier and parts master data for procurement and service fulfillment
  • Align dealer hierarchies to improve coverage, lead routing, and warranty claims
Retail

Product, customer, and store master data

Retailers depend on clean product and customer records to keep pricing, assortment, and promotions aligned across channels.

  • Unify product records across PLM, ERP, e-commerce, and POS systems
  • Resolve duplicate customer identities across stores, digital channels, and loyalty programs
  • Connect store and supplier hierarchies for inventory, replenishment, and reporting
Healthcare

Patient, provider, and payer master data

Healthcare organizations need trusted identities across care, claims, and revenue operations to improve patient experience and reduce admin friction.

  • Match patient records across EMR, scheduling, billing, and payer systems
  • Standardize provider and location data for credentialing and referral workflows
  • Improves claims accuracy, reimbursement, and care coordination across networks
