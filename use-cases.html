● Use Cases

MDM use cases built for the industries that cannot afford bad data.

Whether the issue is customer duplication, misaligned vendor records, or inconsistent product hierarchies, the business outcome is the same: slower revenue, higher risk, and weaker decisions.

Domains

Master data problems, solved where they matter most.

Singitham brings a single trusted record to the systems that power every critical workflow, from onboarding and compliance to pricing, service, and product operations.

Finance

Customer, vendor, and account master data

Finance teams need a single view of counterparties and accounts to reduce risk, accelerate onboarding, and keep reporting accurate.

  • Unify customer and vendor records across CRM, ERP, and banking systems
  • Resolve duplicate legal entities for KYC, AML, and regulatory reporting
  • Standardize account hierarchies for cash, risk, and treasury workflows
Automobile

Vehicle, dealer, and parts master data

Automotive businesses often have fragmented dealer, supplier, and product records that slow warranty, service, and channel operations.

  • Match vehicle and VIN data across OEM, dealer, and service platforms
  • Clean supplier and parts master data for procurement and service fulfillment
  • Align dealer hierarchies to improve coverage, lead routing, and warranty claims
Retail

Product, customer, and store master data

Retailers depend on clean product and customer records to keep pricing, assortment, and promotions aligned across channels.

  • Unify product records across PLM, ERP, e-commerce, and POS systems
  • Resolve duplicate customer identities across stores, digital channels, and loyalty programs
  • Connect store and supplier hierarchies for inventory, replenishment, and reporting
Healthcare

Patient, provider, and payer master data

Healthcare organizations need trusted identities across care, claims, and revenue operations to improve patient experience and reduce admin friction.

  • Match patient records across EMR, scheduling, billing, and payer systems
  • Standardize provider and location data for credentialing and referral workflows
  • Improve claims accuracy, reimbursement, and care coordination across networks
