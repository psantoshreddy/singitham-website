Founder & CEO

Santosh Papagari founded Singitham after spending 20 years focused specifically on master data management, inside a 30-year career across the broader data landscape.

Over that time he's led multiple end-to-end MDM implementations — the kind of programs where match-and-merge logic, stewardship workflows, and governance all have to work together, not just in a demo. That work sits inside a wider foundation across data integration, data quality, data security, and data distribution patterns, applied across a range of industries with very different data — and very different tolerances for getting it wrong.

Singitham exists because of a pattern he kept seeing: the fundamentals of good MDM haven't changed, but the cost and time to implement it never came down — until agentic AI made it possible to automate the parts that used to require a large team of specialists.
